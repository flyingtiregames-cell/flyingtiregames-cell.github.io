Trashy Panda

A Sneaky Raccoon Arcade Adventure

Trashy Panda is a stealth arcade game for iPhone where you play a raccoon on a midnight snack run. Sneak down the street, raid trash cans for points, dodge motion lights and nosy neighbors, and get your loot home before you get caught.

How It Works

Run down the street and slip up driveways to knock over trash cans and dig for bonus gems. Every three cans you empty raises your multiplier, all the way up to 10x.

Stay out of the spotlights and freeze when the motion lights sweep past. If a neighbor spots you, they'll chase you down the street, so run, use parked cars to lose them, or race to safety.

Get caught and you lose every point you haven't banked. Bank your score at Shaye's Safe Haven to lock it in, or keep pushing your luck for a bigger payout and climb the Game Center leaderboard.

Features

  • Sneak, loot and dodge in a moonlit neighborhood
  • A multiplier that climbs up to 10x
  • Neighbors who chase you when you're spotted
  • Bank your points at Shaye's Safe Haven
  • Game Center leaderboard
  • No ads and no tracking

Sum Voyage

Travel the World One Puzzle at a Time

Sum Voyage is a relaxing number puzzle game for iPhone. Every level takes you to a new city around the world, where you solve a logic puzzle by circling the right numbers and erasing the rest. It's easy to pick up, hard to put down, and made for anyone who loves number puzzles.

Download on the App Store

How It Works

Each board is filled with numbers, and every row, column and colored region has a target. Circle the numbers that add up to each target and erase the ones that don't belong. Every puzzle has one answer you can reach with pure logic, no guessing.

Use the Pick tool to circle a number and the Erase tool to cross one out. A wrong move costs a passport stamp, and you have three per level, so think before you tap.

Boards grow from 4x4 up to 8x8 as you travel. Win the Rookie, Traveler and Voyager tournaments on your way to becoming World Champion, and unlock new outfits for your traveler as you go.

Features

  • Number logic puzzles with one clear answer
  • A new city to visit every level
  • Boards that grow from 4x4 to 8x8
  • Tournaments and a World Champion title
  • Unlockable traveler outfits and achievements
  • No ads, no account and no tracking

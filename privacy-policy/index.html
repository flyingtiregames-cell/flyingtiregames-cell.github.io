Pastee Privacy Policy

Effective: November 15, 2026
Made by: Flying Tire Games (Jordan Westlund)
Contact: flyingtiregames@gmail.com

Pastee is a snippet, GIF, and password vault app for Mac and iPhone. The short version: Pastee has no servers, no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. We never see your snippets, passwords, GIFs, or anything you type.

What Pastee stores, and where

Everything you save stays on your devices. If you use iCloud, it also syncs through your own iCloud account, which Apple runs. We can't see or access it.

  • Snippets are saved on your device and sync through iCloud.
  • GIFs are saved on your device and sync through your private iCloud database.
  • The Vault (passwords and secure notes) is encrypted on your device before it's saved anywhere. Passwords and the keys that unlock your Vault sync through iCloud Keychain, which Apple encrypts end to end. Your notes and the list of logins sync as an encrypted file that only your devices can unlock. Without your master password, recovery key, or Face ID / Touch ID, the Vault can't be read by anyone, including us.
  • Settings are saved on your device.

The Pastee keyboard (iPhone)

The Pastee keyboard asks for "Allow Full Access." Pastee uses it only to:

  • read your saved snippets and GIFs from the Pastee app, and
  • copy a GIF so you can paste it.

Pastee does not record, save, or send anything you type. Letters you type with the Pastee keyboard go only to the app you're typing in.

Camera (iPhone)

The camera is used only when you tap Scan Recovery Kit, to read the QR code on your printed Recovery Kit. Nothing the camera sees is saved or sent anywhere.

Face ID and Touch ID

Face ID and Touch ID are handled entirely by Apple. Pastee only learns whether the check passed. It never receives your face or fingerprint data.

Photos and files

When you add a GIF from Photos or Files, you pick it with Apple's own picker, and Pastee only receives the item you choose. Pastee can't see the rest of your photo library.

When you use Save to Pastee with a link to a GIF, Pastee downloads that GIF straight from the website it's on.

Clipboard

Pastee only reads your clipboard when you ask it to: when a snippet uses {clipboard}, or when you tap Paste a GIF. If Clear copied passwords is on, passwords you copy are removed from the clipboard after 30 seconds.

AI Rewrite and Check Grammar (Mac)

These use Apple Intelligence on your Mac. Your text is processed on your device and is never sent to us.

Sharing and importing

  • Shared password files are encrypted with a share password you choose. Pastee never uploads them. You decide how to send them.
  • CSV imports are read on your device and never uploaded. Pastee reminds you to delete the CSV afterward.
  • Email to Myself (recovery key) opens your own email app. Pastee doesn't send any email.

What we collect

Nothing. Pastee doesn't collect, sell, or share any personal information, and it doesn't use analytics or advertising tools.

Deleting your data

  • Delete snippets, GIFs, passwords, or notes in the app at any time.
  • Reset Vault permanently deletes your Vault, on all synced devices if sync is on.
  • To remove Pastee's iCloud data, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on iPhone, or System Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage on Mac.
  • Deleting the app removes the data saved on that device.

Children

Pastee isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Questions

Email flyingtiregames@gmail.com.

Word Herd Privacy Policy

Word Herd Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Word Herd is a word puzzle game made by Flying Tire Games. It has no ads, no tracking and no account to create, and it stores only what the game needs to work.

What the game saves

Your progress is saved on your device. That includes the levels you have finished, your points, items and hearts, the farmer you chose, and your sound settings.

If you are signed in to iCloud, the game also backs up your progress to your own iCloud account, so it can be restored on a new device or after reinstalling. This backup is private to your Apple account. Flying Tire Games cannot see it.

Game Center, the leaderboard and friends

Word Herd uses Apple's Game Center for its leaderboard and for sending hearts between friends. These features are optional, and they only work if you are signed in to Game Center.

  • Leaderboard: the number of levels you have finished is sent to Apple's Game Center and shown beside your Game Center name.
  • Friends list: if you give permission, the game reads your Game Center friends list to show which friends also play Word Herd. The game does not keep a copy of your friends list.
  • Hearts: so that friends can see when you need a heart and send you one, the game stores a small record in its database on Apple's iCloud servers. The record holds your Game Center player ID for this game, your Game Center display name, whether you currently need a heart, and the date that last changed. When a heart is sent, a second record holds the sender's player ID and display name, the receiver's player ID, and the date.

Your friends' copies of the game read these records to show that you need a heart and to deliver hearts. Flying Tire Games can access them. They are used only to run the hearts feature. They are not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Apple's handling of Game Center and iCloud data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game sets a reminder on your device to tell you when a heart has refilled. The reminder is created and delivered by your own device. Nothing is sent to Flying Tire Games to make it work.

What Word Herd doesn't do

  • It shows no ads.
  • It uses no advertising, analytics or tracking services.
  • It does not ask for your real name, email address, location, contacts or photos.
  • It does not sell your information or share it with other companies.

Children

Word Herd is a general-audience game and does not ask anyone for personal information. The leaderboard and hearts features are only available to accounts that Apple allows to use Game Center. If you are a parent or guardian and would like a child's hearts record removed, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Game Center: sign out of Game Center, or decline access to your friends list, and the game will not send a leaderboard score or store a hearts record. The rest of the game still works.
  • iCloud backup: turn off iCloud for Word Herd in the Settings app and your progress stays on your device only. You can remove an existing backup under Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Storage.
  • Notifications: turn them off for Word Herd in the Settings app at any time.
  • Deleting your data: deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. To have your hearts records deleted from the game's database, contact us with your Game Center display name.

Dogzzle

The Dog Logic Puzzle Game for Dog Lovers

Dogzzle is a relaxing logic puzzle game for iPhone where every puzzle is full of dogs. Use clear rules and careful thinking to find where each pup is hiding, solve grid after grid, and collect a whole pack of adorable breeds along the way.

Download on the App Store

How It Works

Every board is split into colored regions, and exactly one dog belongs in each one. Dogs can't share a row or a column, and they can't touch each other, not even diagonally. Use those rules to work out where every dog is hiding.

Mark spots you're unsure of with a question mark, cross out spots that can't hold a dog, and place each pup when you're certain. One wrong guess ends the puzzle, so think it through.

With over 1,000 levels in Easy, Medium and Hard, a breed-themed weekly challenge, achievements and a Game Center leaderboard, there's always another puzzle waiting.

Features

  • Over 1,000 dog logic puzzles in three difficulties
  • 22 adorable dog breeds to collect
  • A new breed-themed challenge every week
  • Achievements and a Game Center leaderboard
  • Relaxing, colorful art and cheerful sound effects

How to Play Element Clash

A complete guide to Element Clash, the elemental orb shooter starring Herb and Dotty. Learn both game modes, memorize the counter chart, see a big combo shot step by step, and pick up the tips that make Hard mode manageable.

By Flying Tire Games · Updated November 20, 2026

The basics in 10 seconds

  1. Drag on the board to aim, then tap to launch your orb.
  2. Classic Mode: touch 3 or more orbs of the same type to clear them.
  3. Counter Mode: launch a stronger element next to a weaker one to clear it.
  4. Orbs that lose their connection to the ceiling fall, and that's how big combos happen.

Clear the whole board to win the level.

Aiming and launching

Herb holds the next orb at the bottom of the screen. Drag your finger on the board to aim, then tap to launch. The orb flies in a straight line and sticks to the first orb it touches. Check the next-orb preview so you can plan one shot ahead.

You can turn on the aimer, a guide line that shows exactly where your shot will go. It's great for learning, but runs played with the aimer don't count for the global leaderboard (more on that below).

Classic Mode

Classic is a traditional orb shooter with an elemental twist, and it's where everyone starts.

  • Match 3 or more. Launch an orb so it touches two or more orbs of the same type, and the whole group clears.
  • Water clears Fire. Water has a special advantage in Classic: launch a Water orb next to Fire and the Fire clears, no match needed.
  • Everything else just matches. Plant, Electric, Boulder and Normal orbs act like regular matching orbs.
  • Loose orbs fall. Any orbs no longer connected to the ceiling drop away and score points.

Counter Mode and the counter chart

Counter Mode throws out matching completely. Orbs only clear when a stronger element is launched next to a weaker one. When that happens, the whole connected group of the weaker type clears at once, and your orb is used up.

🔥 Fire🌱 Plant
🌱 Plant💧 Water
⚡ Electric💧 Water
🪨 Boulder⚡ Electric

A few things to remember:

  • Water is your strongest attacker: it beats two elements, Fire and Boulder.
  • Water is also the easiest target: Plant and Electric both beat it.
  • The elements loop. Water beats Boulder, Boulder beats Electric, and Electric beats Water.
  • A shot that doesn't counter anything just sticks to the board, the same as a miss.
  • Normal orbs don't appear in Counter Mode.

Walkthrough: one shot, nine orbs

The real secret to Element Clash is that clearing a group also drops everything hanging below it. Here's a small Counter Mode board to show how that works.

Step 1: Look at what's holding what

The 5 Fire orbs at the bottom aren't touching the ceiling. They're hanging from the Water group above them. If the Water goes, the Fire goes with it.

You could launch a Water orb at the Fire and clear 5 orbs. But your next orb is Plant, and Plant beats Water. That's the bigger play.

Step 2: Hit the anchor

Aim up the open left side so your Plant orb lands next to the Water. The whole Water group clears, and the Fire has nothing left to hang on to, so it all falls. That's 9 orbs from one shot.

Step 3: Clean up

Now only the top row is left. A Boulder orb next to the Electric group clears all 3 Electric orbs, and a Water orb next to the Boulders finishes the board.

On a real board the same idea scales up. Always look for the group that's holding up the most orbs, not just the biggest group you can see.

The danger meter

Every shot that doesn't clear anything fills the danger meter at the top of the screen. When it fills, the board pushes down closer to Herb and Dotty. Making orbs fall empties the meter again, so dropping groups is how you stay safe. If the orbs reach the bottom, the level is over.

Tips and strategies

1. Go for anchors, not just groups

Find the orbs that connect big sections to the ceiling. Clearing one small anchor group can drop a dozen orbs, and every dropped orb scores the same 10 points as one you hit directly.

2. Use the next-orb preview

Before every shot, glance at the next orb. If your current orb has no good target, place it somewhere harmless and set up a big play for the orb after it.

3. Park bad shots out of the way

A shot that doesn't clear anything sticks to the board. Put it where it won't bury something you need, ideally against a group it can help you match (Classic) or next to an element that will counter it away later (Counter).

4. Learn the counter chart by heart

In Counter Mode, the right orb for every target becomes automatic with practice. A handy trick: Water beats the two "hard" elements (Fire and Boulder), and everything that beats Water is either green or yellow (Plant and Electric).

5. Work from the sides

Orbs near the edges are often what's holding up the outside of the board. Clearing the edges opens lanes so you can reach the middle and the anchors higher up.

6. Play fast for bonus points

Clearing a level in 45 shots or fewer earns a speed bonus, and 35 or fewer earns an even bigger one. Big drops are the fastest way to get there.

Difficulties and unlocks

Both modes have Easy, Medium and Hard difficulties. Harder boards are bigger and more mixed up, and there's less room for misses.

  • Classic Easy is open from the start.
  • Winning levels unlocks Classic Medium, then Classic Hard.
  • Beating Classic Hard unlocks Counter Mode.
  • Counter Mode unlocks its own Medium and Hard the same way.

Scoring and the leaderboard

  • 10 points for every orb cleared, including orbs that fall.
  • 100 point bonus for clearing the level.
  • Speed bonus: +250 for clearing a level in 45 shots or fewer, +500 for 35 or fewer.

Your points add up across every level in one run, and the total goes to the Game Center leaderboard when you return to the menu. To keep the leaderboard fair, runs that use the aimer aren't submitted. Turn the aimer off when you're ready to chase a high score.

Power-ups

Your points also work as currency in the Store. Spend them on power-ups for tough boards:

  • Bomb Orb (500 points): blows up every orb in a radius.
  • Type Freeze (800 points): blocks one element type for 10 shots.
  • Type Erase (1,000 points): removes every orb of one type from the board.

Achievements and the ending

Element Clash has achievements for dropping lots of orbs at once, matching Normal orbs, reaching big point totals, beating Hard levels, winning without the aimer and buying each power-up.

Complete 15 levels to unlock the End Scene achievement and a five-page comic showing how Herb and Dotty's adventure ends. You can rewatch it any time from the Achievements screen.

FAQ

What beats what in Element Clash?

Water beats Fire and Boulder. Fire beats Plant. Plant beats Water. Electric beats Water. Boulder beats Electric. Normal orbs have no counters and don't appear in Counter Mode.

What is the difference between Classic and Counter Mode?

In Classic Mode you match 3 or more orbs of the same type to clear them, and Water also clears Fire. In Counter Mode there is no matching at all: orbs only clear when a stronger element is launched next to a weaker one.

How do I unlock Counter Mode and harder difficulties?

Start with Classic Easy. Winning levels unlocks the next difficulty, and beating Classic Hard unlocks Counter Mode. Counter Mode then unlocks its own Medium and Hard difficulties the same way.

Why didn't my score go on the leaderboard?

Scores only go to the Game Center leaderboard when you play without the aimer. If the aimer was on during your run, your points still count in the game but are not submitted.

What happens when orbs aren't connected to the ceiling?

They fall. Any orbs that lose their connection to the top of the board drop off and count toward your score, which is the best way to clear lots of orbs at once.

Does Element Clash have ads?

No. Element Clash has no ads.

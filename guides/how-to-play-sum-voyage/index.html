How to Play Sum Voyage

Everything you need to travel the world in Sum Voyage: the rules, how Pick and Erase work, a full step-by-step solved city, the strategies that crack big 8x8 boards, and how tournaments and outfits unlock.

By Flying Tire Games · Updated November 20, 2026

The rules in 10 seconds

  1. Every row, column and colored region has a target number.
  2. Circle the numbers that add up to each target.
  3. Erase every number that doesn't belong.
  4. A wrong move costs a passport stamp, and you have 3 per city.

Every puzzle has exactly one answer, and you never need to guess.

The rules explained

Every level in Sum Voyage is a new city with a grid of numbers from 1 to 9. Some of those numbers are part of the answer and the rest are distractors.

  • Rows: the number at the start of each row is its target. The circled numbers in that row must add up to it exactly.
  • Columns: the number at the top of each column works the same way.
  • Colored regions: each region has its own target too, shown in its corner. Its circled numbers must add up to that.

Every row, column and region has at least one circled number, and all three kinds of targets have to work at the same time. That's what makes it a logic puzzle and not just adding up.

Pick, Erase and passport stamps

◯ Pick
Circles a number you know is part of the answer. Tapping a circled number again un-circles it for free.
✕ Erase
Crosses out a number you know doesn't belong.

Both tools are checked right away. If you circle a number that isn't in the answer, or erase one that is, you lose a passport stamp and the move is undone. You get 3 stamps per city. Lose all three and you can tap Try Again to replay the same city with the same numbers.

A city is solved only when every correct number is circled and every other number is erased. Circling the right answers isn't enough on its own.

Tip: Watch the small running totals next to each row and column. They show what your circled numbers add up to so far, so you always know how much is left.

Full walkthrough: solving a city step by step

Here's a 4x4 puzzle, the size of your first ten cities. Rows are numbered 1 to 4 from top to bottom and columns 1 to 4 from left to right. Circled numbers are outlined, erased numbers are faded, and new moves in each step have a white border.

Step 1: Erase numbers that are too big

The yellow region's target is 8. The 9 in row 1 is in the yellow region, and 9 is already bigger than 8, so it can never be part of the answer. Erase it.

Step 2: Look for a line that adds up exactly

Row 3's target is 20. Add up the whole row: 1 + 7 + 6 + 6 = 20. Every number is needed, so circle all four.

Step 3: Count what's left

Column 2's target is 9, and the 7 we just circled already counts toward it. That leaves only 2. The other two open numbers in column 2 are 6 and 8, and both are bigger than 2, so erase them. (The 2 in row 2 will fill the gap.)

Step 4: Row 1 falls into place

Row 1's target is 15. Its only open numbers now are 7 and 8, and 7 + 8 = 15. Circle both.

Step 5: Clear out a finished region

The 8 we just circled is in the yellow region, and the yellow target is 8. The region is complete, so its last open number, the 6 in row 2, gets erased.

Steps 6 and 7: Finish the board

Row 2's target is 8, and its open numbers are 2 + 2 + 4 = 8. Circle all three. Row 4's target is 17, and its open numbers are 6 + 7 + 4 = 17. Circle those too. Every number is now circled or erased, and the city is solved!

Quick check: column 4 is 8 + 4 + 6 + 4 = 22, and the green region is 7 + 6 + 6 + 7 = 26. Every target matches, and we never guessed once.

Solving strategies

1. Erase anything bigger than what's left

If a number is bigger than the remaining target of its row, column or region, it can't be in the answer. On a fresh board, check the smallest targets first. They usually knock out lots of numbers.

2. Look for lines that add up exactly

If every open number in a row, column or region adds up to exactly what's left of its target, circle them all.

3. When a target is met, erase the rest

As soon as a row, column or region reaches its target, every other open number in it gets erased. This is the move you'll make most often.

4. Find the only combination

Say a row needs 10 and its open numbers are 9, 6, 4 and 3. The only way to make 10 is 6 + 4, so circle those and erase the 9 and the 3. On bigger boards, list the combinations that work. A number that's in every combination must be circled, and a number that's in none of them must be erased.

5. Use regions to break ties

When a row has two possible combinations, check the regions those numbers belong to. Often only one combination fits the region's target too.

6. Work with the running totals

The live subtotal badges do the adding for you. Subtract the subtotal from the target and you know exactly what each line still needs.

Points and hints

Every city you solve earns 25 points. If you're stuck, a hint costs 50 points and erases one number that isn't part of the answer. Hints never circle anything for you, so the solve is still yours.

Your traveler and unlockable outfits

You start by creating your traveler and picking a look. As you progress, new outfits unlock:

  • Level 10: Gothic
  • Level 20: Knight
  • Level 30: Karate
  • Level 40: Pilot
  • Level 50: Athlete
  • Level 60: Swim

You can change your name and outfit any time from Settings without losing any progress.

Achievements and the leaderboard

Sum Voyage has achievements for winning each tournament, for solving 25, 50 and 100 cities, for solving 5 cities without losing a single stamp (Flawless Traveler), and for unlocking every outfit (Full Wardrobe). Your score also goes to the Game Center leaderboard after every city, so you can see how you compare with players around the world.

FAQ

Do you ever have to guess in Sum Voyage?

No. Every puzzle is checked by a solver before you see it, so it has exactly one answer and you can always reach it with logic.

Do I have to erase the wrong numbers, or just circle the right ones?

Both. A city is only solved when every correct number is circled and every other number is erased.

What happens if I lose all three passport stamps?

The level ends and you can tap Try Again. You get the same city and the same numbers again, with all three stamps back.

How do hints work in Sum Voyage?

A hint costs 50 points and erases one number that isn't part of the answer. It never circles anything for you, so you still solve the puzzle yourself.

How big do the boards get?

Boards start at 4x4 and grow every 10 levels: 5x5 at level 11, 6x6 at level 21, 7x7 at level 31 and 8x8 from level 41 on.

Does Sum Voyage have ads?

No. Sum Voyage has no ads, no account and no tracking.

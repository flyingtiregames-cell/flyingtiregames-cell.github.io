The Adventures of Flying Tire

A Sky-High Arcade Dodge Game

Flap, dive and dodge your way through the sky in The Adventures of Flying Tire, a fast and funny arcade game for iPhone. You're a tire with wings and a satchel, soaring through the clouds while swarms of bees and woodpeckers fly straight at you. How far can you go before one of them gets you?

Download on the App Store

How It Works

Tap to flap and swipe to dive. Move up and down to slip through the gaps as bees and woodpeckers come flying in from ahead. It takes seconds to learn, but staying alive is another story.

Everything slowly speeds up the longer you survive, so every run gets more intense. One hit and you're out, unless you've grabbed a shield. Pick one up and Flying Tire turns gold, giving you one extra hit before it's game over.

Every run is a chance to beat your best. Push your score higher, then see how you stack up against other players on the Game Center leaderboard.

Features

  • Simple controls: tap to flap, swipe to dive
  • Dodge swarms of bees and woodpeckers
  • Speed that keeps building the longer you survive
  • Shields that turn Flying Tire gold and save you from a hit
  • Game Center leaderboard
  • No ads and no tracking

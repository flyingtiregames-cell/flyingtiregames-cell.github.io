Word Herd

Sort the Words, Pen the Sheep

Word Herd is a cozy word puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. Every sheep carries a word, and every word belongs to a hidden category. Swap the sheep until each row holds four words that go together, pen the flock, and travel across five farm worlds with your farmer and trusty sheepdog Norb.

Download on the App Store

How It Works

Each board has rows of four words scrambled across several categories, like colors, moon phases or vegetables. Tap one sheep and then another to swap them. When a row holds four words from the same category, the row is penned and a new one appears.

Play as Lucas or Shelly and walk the map from stone to stone through the sky, forest, meadow, snowy mountains and farm. At the end of every board, race a wolf boss: solve categories to drain its health before it drains yours.

Visit the hay bale shop to trade points for Hints, Word Pairs, Category Erases and extra hearts, take on weekly picture puzzles and monthly challenges, climb the Game Center leaderboard and send hearts to friends.

Features

  • 1,000 levels across five farm worlds
  • Wolf boss battles at the end of every board
  • Weekly picture puzzles and monthly challenges
  • Hay bale shop with hints and power-ups
  • Game Center leaderboard and hearts to send to friends
  • Plays on iPhone and iPad, no ads

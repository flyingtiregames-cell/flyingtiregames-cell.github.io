Tag Team Titan

An Endless Runner Starring Herb and Dotty

Herb and Dotty are back on Titan, and this time the moon itself won't let them leave. Tag Team Titan is a fast-paced endless runner for iPhone and iPad where you switch between two heroes on the fly. Blast through walls, leap onto high ledges and grab glowing element orbs as you race to escape.

Download on the App Store

How It Works

Herb and Dotty run together, and whoever is in front is in control. Swipe up to swap. With Herb in front, tap to fire his laser and blast through walls. With Dotty in front, tap to jump over obstacles and onto high rock shelves, or swipe down to roll.

Swipe left and right to change lanes and line up the right hero for what's coming. Some stretches force you to shoot, others force you to jump, and the best rewards sit behind the riskiest moves. Power-ups wait on the highest shelves: a magic wand that doubles your points and a shield that saves you from a hit.

Race across five wild locations, from Titan's rocky surface to underground caves, an icy mountain, an electric forest and a flame canyon, each with its own alien creatures and element orbs. Take on the weekly challenge, chase high scores on the Game Center leaderboard and unlock all 16 achievements.

Features

  • Two heroes in one run: swap between Herb and Dotty anytime
  • Simple swipe-and-tap controls
  • Five locations, each with its own aliens and element orbs
  • Magic wand x2 multiplier and shield power-ups
  • Weekly challenge, 16 achievements and a leaderboard
  • Plays on iPhone and iPad

Mad Manor Escape

A Haunted Escape Room Puzzle Adventure

Three siblings. One haunted manor. Mad Manor Escape is an escape room puzzle game for iPhone where Zeek, MeMe and Monty wake up trapped in different corners of a creepy old mansion. Explore every room, crack its puzzles and free each other, all while something strange stirs in the halls.

Download on the App Store

How It Works

Each sibling starts locked in a different part of the manor: Zeek in the dusty attic, MeMe in the dark basement, and Monty in the cluttered garage. Use the joystick to explore, double-tap to inspect objects, and search every corner for clues, keys and hidden items.

Every room is packed with puzzles. Decode riddles, play a melody on an old piano, beat a retro arcade game, crack combination locks and find secrets hidden in paintings and maps. Solve the right puzzle and you'll free another sibling.

Switch between Zeek, MeMe and Monty to use each sibling's special skill, and work together across the manor to uncover its secrets, and whatever is haunting it.

Features

  • Three playable siblings, each with a special skill
  • A full haunted manor to explore, from attic to basement
  • Riddles, codes, music, memory and arcade-style puzzles
  • Hidden clues in paintings, windows, books and maps
  • Comic-style story scenes
  • Moody art and spooky background music

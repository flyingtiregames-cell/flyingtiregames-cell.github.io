Phrase Pickle

The Hilarious Team Guessing Game

Phrase Pickle - Face Off is a fast, loud and hilarious party game for iPhone. Split into two teams, describe the word on screen without saying it, and pass the phone before time runs out. Whoever is holding it when the buzzer goes off is in a pickle.

Download on the App Store

How It Works

One player holds the phone and describes the word or phrase to their team without saying it. When the team guesses it, tap Got it! and pass the phone to the other team, who get a new word to describe. The timer is hidden, so nobody knows when the buzzer will go off.

In Classic Pickle, each team starts with 5 points and loses one every time they're caught holding the phone. Rapid Fire gives each team 30 seconds to get through as many words as they can. The Whole Pickle adds a last-chance Save Your Pickle showdown.

Shake things up with power-ups: a Death Word sends the other team a tough one, Time Boost adds 10 seconds, and Double Up makes the other team describe two words at once.

Features

  • Three game modes: Classic Pickle, Rapid Fire and The Whole Pickle
  • Power-ups: Death Word, Time Boost and Double Up
  • Hidden timer with speeding-up beeps
  • Perfect for game nights, parties and family get-togethers
  • No account, no ads and no tracking

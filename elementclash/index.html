Element Clash

An Elemental Orb Shooter Adventure!

Get ready to aim, match, counter and clear your way through an exciting puzzle adventure. Element Clash is a colorful, strategy-filled orb shooter that combines classic bubble shooter gameplay with elemental powers, challenging levels and two game modes.

Download on the App Store

How It Works

Join Herb, a brilliant but quirky scientist, and Dotty, his clever orangutan companion, after their spaceship crashes on the mysterious world of Titan. Their mission: collect enough Elemental Energy to repair the ship and find their way home.

In Classic Mode, aim and launch orbs to match three or more of the same element, set off chain reactions and drop whole clusters. Water, Fire, Plant, Electric and Rock orbs each add their own twist.

In Counter Mode there's no matching at all. Orbs only clear when a stronger element hits a weaker one, like Water putting out Fire or Plant soaking up Water. Every shot takes strategy, not luck. Win to unlock harder difficulties, then take on the global leaderboard.

Features

  • Classic Mode with match-3 chain reactions
  • Counter Mode where elements beat each other
  • Easy, Medium and Hard difficulties to unlock
  • Global Game Center leaderboard and achievements
  • Herb and Dotty's story on the moon Titan
  • Plays on iPhone and iPad
